Section I

Methodology & Metrics

Execution Model

Signals are computed at the close of bar t and executed at the open of bar t+1. Directional slippage is applied (buys pay more, sells receive less). Commission is charged on notional traded. Flat targets close the position exactly, without residual dust. Warmup bars are treated as flat.

Benchmark

The benchmark is a passive long position in the underlying asset, scaled to the same starting capital as the strategy. It does not charge transaction costs. Same-exposure comparisons in the walk-forward section charge the strategy's average turnover cost to the passive alternative.
