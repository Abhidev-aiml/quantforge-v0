Milestone 4 — Completion Summary

Project: QuantForge v0
Milestone: 04
Status: ✅ Completed

Objective

Milestone 4 established the analytics and performance-measurement layer of QuantForge.

The goal was to take the equity curve and completed trades generated by the backtesting engine and convert them into meaningful quantitative performance metrics.

Completed Components
1. Equity-Curve Metrics

Implemented calculation of:

Total return
CAGR
Number of periods
Years represented by the backtest
2. Risk Metrics

Implemented:

Annualized volatility
Downside deviation
3. Risk-Adjusted Metrics

Implemented:

Sharpe ratio
Sortino ratio
Calmar ratio
Annual risk-free-rate adjustment
4. Tail / Distribution Metrics

Implemented:

VaR 95%
CVaR 95%
VaR 99%
CVaR 99%
Skewness
Kurtosis
5. Drawdown Analysis

Implemented:

Maximum drawdown
Average drawdown
Maximum drawdown duration in bars

The drawdown calculation uses the running equity high-water mark to determine underwater periods.

6. Trade-Level Analytics

Implemented:

Number of trades
Win rate
Profit factor
Expectancy
Average winning trade
Average losing trade
Payoff ratio
7. Fill → Trade Conversion

Implemented extract_trades() to convert engine fills into round-trip trades.

The trade extraction tracks:

Entry
    ↓
Position held
    ↓
Exit
    ↓
Round-trip trade

Each extracted trade records:

Entry timestamp
Exit timestamp
Direction
Entry price
Exit price
Quantity
Gross P&L
Commission
Net P&L
8. Floating-Point Tolerance Fix

extract_trades() was made tolerant of floating-point position dust.

Instead of relying on:

position == 0.0

the implementation uses:

abs(position) < 1e-9

This prevents tiny numerical residuals from being incorrectly interpreted as an open position.
